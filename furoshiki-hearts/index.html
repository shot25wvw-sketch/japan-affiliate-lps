Furoshiki for Modern Gifting

Furoshiki is the traditional Japanese technique of wrapping with cloth instead of paper, using simple folds and knots.

A playful pattern like hearts brings the technique into birthdays, anniversaries, and Valentine's gifting, not just formal occasions.
