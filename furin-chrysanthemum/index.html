Why Chrysanthemum Appears So Often in Japanese Design

The chrysanthemum holds a special place in Japanese culture, long associated with autumn, longevity, and the Imperial family's crest.

Pairing that motif with a traditional furin connects the chime to a much older decorative lineage than most summer decor.
