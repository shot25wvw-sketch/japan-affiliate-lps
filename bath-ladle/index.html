The Ritual Behind It

In Japanese bathing culture, you rinse off with a ladle before ever stepping into the tub - the tub itself stays for soaking only, not washing.

A dedicated ladle, called a yu-oke scoop, makes that rinse-first habit easy to actually keep up at home.
