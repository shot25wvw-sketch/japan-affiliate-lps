What Is a Kenzan?

A kenzan, or flower frog, is a heavy pin holder used in ikebana to anchor stems at precise angles - the tool behind that effortless-looking asymmetry.

Paired with a wide, shallow vase, it's the classic starting setup for anyone learning Japanese flower arranging.
