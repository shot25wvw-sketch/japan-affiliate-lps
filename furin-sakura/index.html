Why Sakura Is Furin's Favorite Motif

Cherry blossoms are Japan's most recognized floral symbol, tied to spring but used in decor and design throughout the year.

Painting sakura on the inside of a furin bell is a traditional technique, letting the petals seem to float inside the glass as it catches the light.
