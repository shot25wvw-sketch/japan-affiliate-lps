What Makes a Japanese Chisel Different

Japanese oire nomi chisels use a laminated construction - a thin layer of very hard, high-carbon white steel forge-welded to a softer iron body.

That combination lets the edge get harder and sharper than a single-steel Western chisel, while the softer back keeps it from being brittle - and the hollow-ground back makes it easier to flatten and re-sharpen.
