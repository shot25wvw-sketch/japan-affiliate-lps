Good to Know

What is a noren?

Noren are traditional Japanese fabric dividers, typically hung in doorways at homes and shops — split down the middle so you can walk through.

How do I hang it?

It slides onto a standard curtain rod or tension rod through the fabric sleeve at the top — no drilling or hardware needed.

Does the pattern vary between pieces?

Since it uses a shibori-style dye pattern, slight natural variation between pieces is part of the charm.
