The Work Behind the Garment

Samue is traditional Japanese workwear, originally worn by Zen Buddhist monks for manual labor and later adopted by potters, woodworkers, and other craftspeople for its practical, unrestrictive cut.

Sashiko, the running-stitch technique often used on samue, was developed to reinforce and insulate work clothing - function first, with the distinctive stitched texture as a byproduct.
