Where the Gold-Seam Look Comes From

The gold crack pattern on these mugs is styled after kintsugi, the Japanese practice of repairing broken pottery with gold rather than hiding the damage.

Built into a brand-new mug, that same golden-seam aesthetic becomes a daily reminder of the philosophy, without needing to repair anything yourself.
