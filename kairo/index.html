A Small Habit Japanese Winters Run On

Kairo are disposable, air-activated warmers that have been a staple of Japanese winters for decades - stuck to the lower back, stomach, or inside a coat for hours of steady warmth.

Unlike a bulky hand warmer, the stick-on style stays put through a full day outside, which is part of why they've stayed a go-to over space heaters and electric blankets for portable warmth.
