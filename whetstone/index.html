Sharpening in 3 Simple Steps

A quick weekly habit that makes a real difference in the kitchen.

Soak

Submerge the stone in water for a few minutes before use.

Sharpen

Glide the blade across the coarse side at a consistent angle, then flip to the fine side.

Rinse

Wipe the blade clean and let the stone air dry before storing.

Good to Know

What do the grit numbers mean?
