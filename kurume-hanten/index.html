Where the Kurume Stripe Comes From

Kurume, in Japan's Fukuoka prefecture, has a centuries-old reputation for indigo-dyed striped cotton textiles (kurume-gasuri), a tradition this coat's pattern draws from.

A handmade hanten in this style carries that regional textile history into a genuinely wearable winter coat, rather than treating the pattern as decoration alone.
