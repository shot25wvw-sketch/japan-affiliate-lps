The Suribachi Difference

A suribachi is a traditional Japanese mortar with a ridged, unglazed interior, paired with a wooden surikogi pestle - a different design from a smooth Western mortar and pestle.

Those ridges are what let it release the oil from sesame seeds for a fresher, more aromatic result than simply crushing them.
